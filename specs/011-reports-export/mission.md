# Reports & Export Module — Mission

## Purpose

The Reports & Export module transforms raw fitness data into actionable
insights and portable formats. It answers the user's most important
questions: "How am I doing?" and "Can I take this data with me?"

## Users

**Primary**: Individual fitness-tracking app users who want to understand
their workout patterns, nutrition habits, and progress over time — and
export that understanding for personal analysis, sharing with coaches,
or record-keeping.

**Secondary**: Users who need printable or spreadsheet-ready versions of
their fitness data for external use (healthcare providers, personal
archives, fitness challenges).

## What This Module Delivers

1. **Visibility**: A dedicated Reports page with an at-a-glance Fitness
   Overview plus deep-dive Workout, Nutrition, and Progress reports.
2. **Control**: Date-range selection (presets + custom) that drives every
   report and export consistently.
3. **Portability**: Accurate CSV and PDF exports that faithfully represent
   what the user sees on screen — no drift, no fabrication.
4. **Trust**: Every metric derived from real, persisted data; gaps rendered
   as explicit "no data" states; exports that match the on-screen view
   exactly.

## What This Module Does NOT Do

- It does NOT create, modify, or delete any fitness data.
- It does NOT estimate, approximate, or fabricate missing values.
- It does NOT share, publish, or email reports externally.
- It does NOT add new data collection (no new model fields, no new
  tracking features).

## Success Looks Like

A user opens Reports, sees a clear summary of their last 30 days, drills
into any report type, changes the date range, and exports a CSV or PDF
that they can open in Excel or print — all within seconds, all accurate,
all private to them.
