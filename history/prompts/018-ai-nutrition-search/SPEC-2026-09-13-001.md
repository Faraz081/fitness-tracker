## Prompt

Nutrition Search (AI-Powered Food Logging). A dedicated AI-powered nutrition search and food-logging feature where users type what they ate or want to eat using natural language, the system looks up real nutrition data via a live AI (Gemini) backend call, and shows a result card with full macros. Users can then add the food to a meal section of their daily nutrition log. The core philosophy: every search hits live AI — there is no static/hardcoded source of truth behind the results, and the AI result is never shown as authoritative (the app is a calculator, the AI is the assistant). Users are never asked to manually enter macros or food search keywords — they type plain language like "grilled chicken sandwich with cheese" or "2 slices of toasts and a glass of orange juice". Results appear as a single result card showing: food name, "AI" badge, "Per Xg serving" label, and Calories | Protein | Carbs | Fat, plus the optional micronutrients Fiber | Sugar | Sodium. A "+" button opens the Add-to-Log modal that pre-fills quantity (grams) from the AI result, lets the user edit the quantity, shows live recalculation of macros "For [X]g: [Y] kcal | P | C | F" derived strictly from the AI's real data, and offers a meal section dropdown (Breakfast/Lunch/Dinner/Snack). Cancelling closes the modal; confirming persists to the real DB (owner-scoped, tied to the authenticated user) so the added food appears instantly under the selected meal section in real time (no refresh). Any meal section with zero logged foods shows the honest empty state "No food logged for [Meal]" with a hint "Use AI search above to find and add foods." Loading and error states during AI calls (fail gracefully, no fabricated numbers) are required. Security: the AI key lives only on the backend; every call routes through the authenticated backend endpoint; all saved entries are scoped to the logged-in user, strictly isolated from other users.
